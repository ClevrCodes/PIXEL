What’s weighing

Explore a pressure to see how it can show up in student life. These experiences are connected—and no two stories are exactly alike.

Showing 8 pressures

01↗

STUDY & PERFORMANCE

Academic
expectations

The pace of coursework, high-stakes exams, grades and the sense that every result says something about your potential.

Look closer +

Assignments can stack up while the standards—your own or someone else’s—keep rising. The pressure may be about keeping up, standing out, or feeling that there is no room to make a mistake.

02◌

MONEY & SECURITY

Financial
strain

Tuition, rent, food, transport and the daily calculations that can make money hard to set aside.

Look closer +

Financial pressure may shape where a student lives, how much they work, which opportunities feel possible, and how secure the next month seems.

03⌁

WHAT COMES NEXT

Future
uncertainty

Choosing a direction while the path ahead is still unclear—and watching the clock seem to move faster.

Look closer +

Questions about careers, identity and what comes after graduation can make the present feel like a test for a future that has not taken shape yet.

04⌘

PEOPLE & CONNECTION

Belonging
& friendship

Finding a place in a new community, forming friendships and figuring out where you fit.

Look closer +

Campus life can be full of people and still feel lonely. Social comparison, changing friendships and the wish to be understood can all be part of the experience.

05◷

TIME & ENERGY

Too much
to hold

Classes, paid work, caring responsibilities and personal commitments competing for the same hours.

Look closer +

When responsibilities overlap, rest and unstructured time may become scarce. A packed schedule can leave little space between one demand and the next.

06⌂

HOME & EXPECTATIONS

Family
pressures

Carrying family hopes, responsibilities or worries—nearby, from a distance, or across cultures.

Look closer +

Family can be a source of connection and expectation at the same time. Students may feel pulled between their own choices and what people at home need or imagine for them.

07✳

SELF & COMPARISON

The pressure
to measure up

Comparing progress, appearance or achievements—and wondering whether you are doing enough.

Look closer +

Other people’s milestones can feel like a measuring stick. Perfectionism and the pressure to appear as though everything is going well may make uncertainty harder to show.

08⤴

CHANGE & TRANSITION

Everything
is changing

Adjusting to a new place, new routines and a different sense of independence—often all at once.

Look closer +

Transitions can bring excitement alongside disorientation. Even changes that are chosen may involve grief for familiar routines, people or versions of yourself.
